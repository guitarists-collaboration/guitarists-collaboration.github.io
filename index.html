Fretboard Visualization

24-Fret Interactive Neck

See exactly what is happening in the music. View active playing chord shapes overlaid seamlessly on scale and modal intervals with radiant note illumination and audio-reactive peaks.

  • Simultaneous chord-tone and scale-tone mapping
  • Dynamic audio volume glow bursts on active chords
  • Adjustable interval, finger, and note-name displays
  • Interactive fret-by-fret chord voice exploration
Rhythm Telemetry

Industrial Drum Radar Visualizer

A precision 16-tap vector scope that replaces cluttered circles with a clean, CAD-inspired telemetry display. Watch drum strikes flash with voice-colored LEDs and vector sweep needle precision.

  • Dedicated color codes for Kick, Snare, Hi-Hats, and Cymbals
  • Industrial step indices and quadrant subdivision markers
  • Zero bezel obstruction with flush scope aperture
  • Synchronized real-time tempo and bar telemetry
Audio Synthesis

Studio Channel Mixer & Automation

Mix your rhythm section with precision faders. Layer secondary chords and rhythm instruments, adjust master reverb and chorus acoustics, and program automated instrument shifts per bar.

  • Individual channel control for Drums, Bass, Chords, and Layer 2
  • Acoustic Reverb and Chorus depth controls
  • Per-bar automation of rhythm patterns and instrument voices
  • Responsive VU meters with real-time level monitoring
Songwriting

Tactile Progression Editor & Library

Quickly build custom backing tracks bar-by-bar. Select roots, qualities, extensions, and strumming grooves, then save them directly to your personal device track library.

  • Complete library of rock, blues, jazz, reggae, and acoustic grooves
  • Instant QR code generation to share your tracks with students or bandmates
  • Offline storage—never lose access to your practice tracks
