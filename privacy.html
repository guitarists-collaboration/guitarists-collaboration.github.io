Privacy Policy

UPDATED: SEPTEMBER 2026

Our Core Promise: Zero Data Collection

At Guitarists Collaboration, our focus is entirely on music education, fretboard mastery, and songwriting tools. We believe your creative work, practice habits, and musical compositions belong exclusively to you.

  • No Accounts Required: You never need to create a profile or log in to use our apps.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not track user behavior or device fingerprinting.
  • No Ad Trackers: Our tools do not sell or transmit behavioral data to third-party ad brokers.

Local Device Storage

All track compositions, custom chord progressions, mixer levels, and exercise sessions created in Guitar Studio are stored strictly in your device's private local storage.

Your audio and compositions are never uploaded or synced to external servers unless you explicitly choose to export or share a track with a fellow musician.

Microphone & Audio Permissions

If you use the built-in instrument tuner, the app requests local microphone access solely to analyze audio frequencies in real-time. No audio is ever recorded, saved, or transmitted across the internet.

Track Sharing & Links

When you share a track via QR code or share link, the track data (chords and rhythm patterns) is compressed directly inside the URL itself. No central database stores your shared tracks—the link itself is the entire song configuration.

Questions & Support

If you have questions regarding this policy or need assistance with Guitar Studio, feel free to reach out to our team at guitarly_app@gmail.com.
