Guitar Studio

Your Interactive Guitar Practice & Backing Track Partner

Transform the way you practice, write, and master the guitar! Guitar Studio places an animated backing band, visual chord library, and scale trainer directly in your pocket. Tired of boring metronomes? Play along with high-fidelity, customizable tracks that adapt to your style and skill level.

🆓 100% Free Core Features: Create tracks, tune your guitar, study scales, and practice chord progressions completely free of charge. No credit card, no sign-ups, and no trial expirations.

Key Features You'll Love
  • 🎯 Precision Chromatic Tuner: Fast, responsive microphone-based tuner right from the navigation menu so you're ready to jam in seconds.
  • 🥁 Build Your Virtual Band: Combine drums, bass lines, and rhythmic guitar guides. Adjust tempo and visualize beats dynamically.
  • 🎛️ Dual-Rhythm Panning & Mixer: Layer two independent rhythm tracks with automatic stereo panning, animated VU meters, reverb & chorus.
  • 🎸 Interactive Fretboard Visualizer: Display chord shapes and scale fingerings (notes, intervals, or fingers) with unified note-highlighting.
  • 🧠 AI-Powered Quick Import: Paste bar-delimited chord sequences (Am | D7 | G | C) from tabs or AI assistants to generate tracks instantly.
  • 🤝 Share & Collaborate: Export progressions, scale guides, and tracks to students or bandmates using quick web links or QR codes.
  • 🚀 Pro Upgrade (One-Time, No Subscriptions): Track embedding (intro/verse/chorus/bridge), focused sub-looping, drum radar display, up to 12 saved tracks.
  • Note: Focuses on backing tracks, rhythm mixing, and tuning (does not offer scale exercises).

Guitar Solo

Lead Soloing Trainer, CAGED & 3NPS Frameworks

Focused lead guitar trainer for mastering scales, modes, and arpeggios over dynamic rhythm tracks. Perfect for learning fretboard geometry, CAGED positions, speed building, and modal improvisation.

🆓 100% Free Core Features: Practice scales, loop custom progressions, and master interval degrees completely free. No sign-ups, no tracking, full offline capability.

Key Features You'll Love
  • 🎼 Interactive Scale Exercises: Structured scale exercises, modal drills, and interval tests to develop fretboard dexterity and speed.
  • 📐 CAGED & 3NPS Frameworks: Visualize pentatonic boxes, 7 diatonic modes, and 3-note-per-string patterns connected seamlessly across 24 frets.
  • ⚡ Real-Time Interval Tracking: Root notes, 3rds, 5ths, and tensions light up dynamically in sync with active chord sequences.
  • 🔁 Chord Progression Looper: Custom backing tracks, adjustable tempo, and targeted transition looping.
  • 🔊 FluidSynth Audio Engine: Low-latency audio rendering with multi-sampled acoustic/electric guitars, bass lines, and acoustic drum grooves.
  • ☀️🌙 Daylight & Dark OLED Themes: High-contrast color palettes designed for crystal-clear readability both outdoors and on stage.
  • Note: Focuses on scale exercises and lead soloing (does not include the built-in guitar tuner).
